## Search and compare

- [CarDeals](/cardeals) compares car prices reported by buyers, including what they paid after taxes and fees.
- [Haven](/haven) screens home listings by climate and compares estimated monthly costs.
- [High Match](/high-match) ranks job openings by profile and explains why each one matched. You can try the hosted preview without signing in.
- [Property Tax Lookup](/property-tax) looks up a property's tax bills and payment history in supported counties.

## Everyday tools

- [ChipDue](/chipdue) tracks card payments, signup bonuses, and account balances in a private workspace.
- [Desk Captions](/desk-captions) turns spoken French into live English captions in your browser or on a Mac.
- [Dialora](/dialora) lets you rehearse a phone conversation in your browser before making the call.
- [Yumoo](/yumoo) turns meal photos into an illustrated food diary and monthly calendar.

## Create and play

- [Keyfall](/keyfall) lets you practice piano with falling notes, adjustable speed, and loops for difficult passages.
- [Mayhem Companion](/mayhem) shows builds and statistics for ARAM Mayhem, Arena, and Teamfight Tactics on Windows.
- [Memeo](/memeo) lets you find a meme template, add a caption, and export the image.

The [interactive portfolio](/portfolio) includes a map of places I've lived, a Wordle game, and other demos.
